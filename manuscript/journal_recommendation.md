# Target-journal recommendation

## Recommended primary target: *Landscape and Urban Planning*

This is the best current fit for the manuscript's combination of urban
land-cover change, constrained spatial allocation, scenario planning and
explicit planning-objective evaluation. The paper should be positioned as a
methodological benchmark and conditional planning framework, not as an
official Abu Dhabi forecast. The public-data limitation is acceptable only if
the abstract, title and discussion retain the present claim boundary.

## Strong alternative: *Computers, Environment and Urban Systems*

This venue is particularly well matched to the Geospatial Kernel as a
state--action--constraint algorithmic core, the execution contract and the
mechanism-control experiments. It may be preferable if the paper is framed
more strongly as urban computing and spatial decision support than as a
land-change case study.

## Data/remote-sensing alternative: *International Journal of Applied Earth Observation and Geoinformation*

This is suitable for the public Earth-observation benchmark, land-cover
crosswalk, multi-year validation and spatial machine-learning components. A
submission should avoid implying that Dynamic World labels are authoritative
legal land-use records.

## Nature-family target: *npj Urban Sustainability*

This is the realistic Nature-family option. The manuscript would need to make
the urban-sustainability relevance explicit and retain the mechanism controls,
uncertainty discussion and public-data boundary. A stronger version with
authoritative local data, independent validation or a second city would
materially improve the case.

## Aspirational target: *Nature Communications*

Direct submission is high risk in the current single-city, public-data form.
The paper lacks authoritative local land-use validation and does not yet
demonstrate transfer across cities or data regimes. It is best treated as a
longer-term target after a broader validation study.

## Suggested submission order

1. *Landscape and Urban Planning* (current primary target)
2. *Computers, Environment and Urban Systems*
3. *International Journal of Applied Earth Observation and Geoinformation*
4. *npj Urban Sustainability* (Nature-family target)
5. *Nature Communications* (aspirational)
