# Nature figure contract

## Common export contract

- Backend: Python / Matplotlib only.
- Primary export: editable SVG with text preserved as text nodes.
- Secondary exports: PDF with editable TrueType text, 600-dpi PNG and TIFF.
- Typeface: Arial with DejaVu Sans and Liberation Sans fallbacks.
- Target size: approximately 183 mm wide for a two-column Nature-style figure.
- No rainbow scales; model colours remain fixed across all quantitative panels.

## Figure 1

- Core conclusion: the Abu Dhabi benchmark gives a FLUS-style ANN–CA console, Geospatial Kernel and GeoFM-LDN the same state, action and constraint contract, while the Kernel exposes proposal, projection and state writeback as inspectable steps.
- Archetype: schematic-led composite.
- Panel map: public inputs and common contract; Kernel execution loop; historical and planning evaluation tracks.
- Review risk: do not imply that public proxy constraints are statutory planning red lines.

## Figure 2

- Core conclusion: historical allocation performance is horizon-dependent, with Geospatial Kernel leading in 2023 and GeoFM-LDN leading in 2024.
- Archetype: quantitative grid.
- Panel map: change FoM, change F1, overall accuracy and macro-F1.
- Statistics: mean ± population standard deviation over seeds 31, 47 and 73.

## Figure 3

- Core conclusion: under the released public-data objective set, the three models trade off road accessibility, prior-built proximity, newly built fragmentation and ecological conversion differently within each scenario.
- Archetype: quantitative grid.
- Panel map: demand error, ecological conversion, compactness, distance to major roads, distance to prior built cells and built retirement.
- Statistics: three-seed means and population standard deviations where available; objective directions are stated in each panel.

## Figure 4

- Core conclusion: the historical and planning differences can be decomposed into learned proposal features and runtime allocation semantics.
- Archetype: quantitative grid with mechanism contrasts.
- Panel map: proposal ablation, runtime-control deltas, planning compactness, and hard-constraint violations.
- Boundary: these are public-data mechanism controls, not causal policy experiments.

## Figure 5

- Core conclusion: the three models produce visibly different 2031 transition footprints under the same scenario targets.
- Archetype: image plate plus categorical transition overlay.
- Panel map: compact, ecological-priority and outward-growth rows crossed with the three models.
- Boundary: pixels are 100-m transition units, not cadastral parcels.
